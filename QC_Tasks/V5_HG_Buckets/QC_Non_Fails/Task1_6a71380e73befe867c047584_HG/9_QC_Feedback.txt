Component: Oracle Events (OEs) - OE Accuracy
Rate OE Accuracy
Auditors should verify OEs against the universe data using the Explorer or Sandbox. Each OE should reference the correct tool, correct service, correct parameters, and correct expected data. An OE that describes "look up Fatimah's relocations in Linear" when relocation records are actually in the CRM is inaccurate — the data exists but the OE points to the wrong place. OE Accuracy is distinct from OE Completeness: completeness checks whether steps are missing; accuracy checks whether the steps that exist are correct.
